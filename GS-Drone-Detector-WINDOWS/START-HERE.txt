GS DRONE DETECTOR v0.9 — QUICK START

INSTALL ON YOUR PC:
1. Double-click GS-Drone-Detector-v0.9-Setup.exe.
2. In your browser, click INSTALL APPLICATION.
3. Click CLOSE SETUP, then open GS Drone Detector from Start Menu.

RUN FROM USB:
1. Copy GS-Drone-Detector-v0.9-Portable.exe onto your flash drive.
2. On a Windows PC, double-click the EXE.
3. In your browser, click START SCANNING.
4. Click EXIT APP before ejecting your USB drive.

No separate setup tools or development software are required to use either EXE.

This version offers Wi-Fi name-based hints, not confirmed drone identification.
See README.md for full details.
